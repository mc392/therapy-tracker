Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

Short version: Therapy Tracker stores everything on your own device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no third party involved. We do not collect, see, or have access to your data or your clients' data — at any point, for any reason.

Who this policy is for

This policy explains how Therapy Tracker ("the app") handles data. It is written for the practitioner who installs and uses the app. If you are a client of a practitioner who uses this app, please direct any question about your personal data to your practitioner directly — see "Data controller" below for why.

What data the app handles

Depending on how you use it, the app may hold information you enter yourself, such as:

  • Client names or codes, contact details, and case notes you choose to record
  • Session dates, attendance, and payment/fee records
  • Room bookings and rates
  • Supervision records
  • Practice branding (name, tagline, colour scheme) and app settings

Where it's stored

All of the above is stored locally in your browser's on-device storage (IndexedDB, with a local-storage safety mirror). Nothing is transmitted anywhere as part of normal use. If you export a backup file, that file is created and saved by your device/browser — the app offers to encrypt it, but the file itself only ever goes where you choose to put it (your own disk, cloud drive, email to yourself, etc.). We never receive a copy.

What we (the developer) collect

Nothing. Specifically, the app has:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No backend server that data is sent to
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or usage telemetry
  • No crash-reporting service
  • No cookies beyond the local storage needed to run the app itself
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs

If any of this ever changes (for example, if we add optional cloud sync in future), this policy will be updated first and the change will be opt-in, never silent.

Data controller

Because your data never leaves your device and we never receive, store, or process it, we are not a data processor and do not act as one under UK GDPR. You, the practitioner, are the data controller for any personal data you enter about your clients, in the same way you would be if you kept paper notes or a spreadsheet. That means the usual professional and legal obligations around client record-keeping, confidentiality, retention, and data subject rights remain yours — the app is a tool you use to meet them, not a party that shares that responsibility.

Security — what's built in, and what's on you

Built in: data stays on-device by design; the app can request persistent storage so the browser is less likely to evict your data; you can export encrypted backups; you can anonymise or permanently erase client records from within the app (Settings → Privacy & removal).

Your responsibility: because everything lives on your device, protecting that device — passcode/biometric lock, disk encryption, not leaving it unlocked or unattended, keeping backup files somewhere secure — is what actually keeps client data safe. Losing an unlocked, unencrypted device is the realistic way this data could be exposed.

Data retention and deletion

Retention is entirely under your control. You can anonymise or erase individual clients, or erase everything, at any time from Settings → Privacy & removal. Nothing is retained by us afterwards, because nothing was held by us in the first place.

Children's data

The app itself is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone. If you use it in a practice that works with minors, the same "you are the controller" principle above applies to that data as to any other client data you record.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes (such as introducing any feature that sends data off-device) will be called out clearly in the app itself before you're asked to use it.
