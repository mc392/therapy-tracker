App icon ideas — Therapy Tracker

Each shown at 180px (iOS home screen), 96px and 48px (worst case).

A — Leaf, refinedYour current mark, rebuilt. Fuller leaf, veins thick enough to survive 48px, subtle vertical gradient. Safest choice — nothing to relearn.
B — Progress ringLeaf inside an almost-closed ring, echoing the goal rings and streak on the Home tab. Reads as "how am I doing" at a glance.
C — Two figuresTherapist and client, one held back in opacity. The most literal "this is a therapy app" mark, and the most distinct from generic finance apps.
D — Growth stepsRising bars with a leaf sprouting off the tallest — tracking plus growth in one shape. Strongest silhouette of the five at small sizes.
E — MonogramLight ground with a ghosted leaf behind a serif "cb" for Charlotte Bloor. The only light icon here, so it stands out in a dark row of apps. Weakest if the practice ever renames.
