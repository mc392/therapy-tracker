Common questions

I used GroundWork in a web browser. Why is the iPhone app empty?

The iPhone app and the web app keep separate copies of your records on your device - nothing is stored on a server, so nothing can sync between them by itself. In the web app, go to Settings → Data & backup → Export backup (.json), save the file somewhere your iPhone can reach (iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself), then in the iPhone app go to Settings → Data & backup → Restore from backup and choose that file.

How do I back up my records?

On iPhone, the simplest way is Settings → This iPhone → Where your records are saved, and choose a folder in iCloud Drive: every change is then written there automatically. Without one, the app keeps automatic copies on the iPhone itself. On any device you can also export a backup file from Settings → Data & backup, and protect it with a passphrase.

I've bought GroundWork Pro or a tax year on a new phone - how do I get it back?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID, open GroundWork, go to Settings → App preferences → What you are paying for, and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not to your records.

How do I cancel my subscription or ask for a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GroundWork. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Your records, and every backup and export, stay free whether or not you subscribe.

Does Pro include working out my tax?

No. GroundWork Pro covers business analytics, costs and other income, accreditation hours, reports and GroundWork Notes sync. Tax calculations are bought separately, one UK tax year at a time; each tax year includes every earlier year and does not expire. Tax figures are estimates to help you plan, not tax advice, and the app cannot file a return for you.

Can you see or recover my records?

No. Everything is stored on your own device; we run no server and never receive a copy. That is why a backup matters - see above. The Privacy Policy explains this in full.
