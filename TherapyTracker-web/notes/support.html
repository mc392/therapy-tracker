Common questions

Which folder should I choose?

For a new vault, choose an empty folder. Put it inside iCloud Drive if you want your notes on more than one device; choose one under "On My iPhone" if you want them on this device only. To open an existing vault on a new device, choose the same folder you picked the first time - the folder itself, not anything inside it.

I've forgotten my passphrase.

On the unlock screen, tap I have forgotten my passphrase and type the recovery key you wrote down when the vault was created, then choose a new passphrase. We hold no copy of your passphrase or your key, so without the recovery key we cannot get your notes back for you.

How do I get my notes onto my iPad or a new iPhone?

Keep the vault in iCloud Drive. On the other device, install GroundWork Notes, choose that same folder, and unlock it with the same passphrase. Notes written on one device appear on the other once iCloud has finished syncing.

Can I bring in the notes I already have?

Yes: Settings → Import existing notes. It reads Word, Excel, CSV, plain text, Markdown, rich text, web pages, Evernote exports and PDFs that hold real text (not scans), and it walks you through getting notes out of Apple Notes. Everything is read on your device and encrypted before it is written, and you see what will be imported before anything is saved.

How do I back up, or get my notes out?

A vault in iCloud Drive is already off your device. For a copy you can open without this app, use Settings → Export everything: every note is written out as a plain text file. That export is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere as protected as the notes themselves.

I corrected a note - why is the original still there?

On purpose. A clinical record should show what was written and when it was changed, so a correction is added alongside the original rather than written over it. Tap "Show corrected" to see both.

Can you see or recover my notes?

No. They are encrypted on your device and the app never contacts us. The Privacy Policy explains this in full.
